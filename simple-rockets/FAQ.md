# FAQ

**Does it replace space platforms?**
No. It's a fast, direct way to send urgent cargo (up to 1 ton per rocket) between planets. Platforms are still the way to move large amounts.

**When can I build it?**
It's **endgame**: it's unlocked after the **cryogenic science pack**, and the rocket parts are made on Vulcanus, Fulgora and Aquilo.

**How do I send something?**
Build a **landing pad** on the planet that needs the items and set its **requests** in the panel on the right of its window. Build a **simple rocket silo** on the planet that has them, inside a logistic network. The silo gathers what the pad is missing, launches, and the rocket lands on the pad a few minutes later.

**Does it respect quality?**
Yes. Request rare plates and rare plates are sent; whatever goes in the rocket arrives with the same quality.

**My silo doesn't launch.**
It never takes off without somewhere to go, and only sends full rockets. Check that a landing pad on **another planet** is missing something (imported from the silo's planet), that the silo's **destination** is Automatic or that pad, and that the silo's logistic network **has** the items. The landing pad's panel tells you what each silo is doing.

**Do logistic robots use the landing pad?**
Only to take things out of it, like a passive provider chest: if something in the network needs what the pad holds, robots pick it up. They never bring anything to it: what it asks for comes by rocket.

**Where can I see my rockets in flight?**
In the **silo** and **landing pad** windows, with their destination and the time left.

**Can I call a rocket back?**
Yes: the cross on a rocket in flight, in the silo's or the landing pad's window. It turns back and lands on the freest landing pad of the planet it left; with none there, its cargo goes back into its silo.

**Why doesn't my silo launch a nearly empty rocket?**
Like the game's launches to space platforms, silos only send **full rockets**: full, or holding everything the pad asks for when that's less. What a pad is missing builds up until it fills a rocket.

**What happens to the rocket after landing?**
It's taken apart into **rocket scrap**. Put the scrap in a recycler to get the rocket's **materials** back: **1 to 3 of each** (heat shield, guidance computer, fuselage and fuel).

**Can I make them faster?**
Yes, with the infinite **Simple rocket speed** research (+15 km/s per level).

**Will it work with Factorio 2.1?**
Yes, it will be updated when 2.1 is out as stable.
