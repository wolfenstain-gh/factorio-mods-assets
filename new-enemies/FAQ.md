# FAQ

**What does it add?**
For now, the **Flyers**: flying biters in four evolution tiers (small, medium, big and behemoth), with their own **flyer nest**. More enemy families are on the way: **Brutes** and **Titans**.

**Where do flyers come from?**
From the **flyer nest**, which shows up next to enemy bases, a bit further from the starting area. Newly explored areas and bases founded by enemy expansion get them too. Its flyers follow the same evolution as biters: small at first, then medium, big and behemoth.

**My walls don't stop them. Is that a bug?**
No, that's the point: flyers fly straight over walls, water and cliffs.

**My turrets don't shoot them. Why?**
Regular turrets can't hit flyers **in the air**; only anti-air turrets can. Once a flyer **lands** to wait or to fight, every turret can shoot it like any biter.

**Which turrets can shoot them in the air?**
**New Turrets (Vanilla+)** brings the **Flak Cannon**, an anti-air turret that only shoots flyers in the air. Turrets from other mods can too, if their author enables it (see "For modders" in the description).

**Do I need New Turrets?**
It isn't required, but it's **recommended**: its defences are designed for these enemies.

**How strong are flyers?**
Their health, damage and resistances follow the vanilla biter of the same tier. What makes them dangerous is that they skip your walls.

**Can I set turret priorities for them?**
Yes. Flyers appear once per tier in the turret **priority list**.

**Do I need Space Age?**
No, it works with the base game.

**Can I add it to an existing save?**
Yes. When you add it, flyer nests are added once next to the enemy bases that already exist, far from the starting area and never within 50 tiles of anything you've built. After that, newly explored areas and bases founded by enemy expansion get them too.

**Can I remove it from a save?**
Yes. The flyers, their nests and their corpses are removed, and every enemy base keeps its vanilla nests: flyer nests are always added next to them, never in their place.
