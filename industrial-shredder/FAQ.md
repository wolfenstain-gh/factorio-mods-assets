# FAQ

**What does it do?**
It destroys whatever you put in it. Items are shredded to nothing and fluids are burned off. Nothing comes out but pollution.

**Does it work with items from other mods?**
Yes. It makes a "Shred X" recipe for every item in the game, including other mods', and picks the right one by itself, like the recycler.

**How do I get rid of fluids?**
Connect a pipe to its fluid input. Any fluid is burned at 200 units per second while it has power, with black smoke from the chimney.

**Do I need Space Age?**
No, it works with or without it.

**Does it freeze on Aquilo?**
Yes, like the recycler: it needs heat there. While frozen it does nothing, and the fluid inside isn't lost.

**Can I get anything back?**
No, that's the point: it's for things you don't want. If you want to recover materials, use the recycler.

**Does it pollute?**
Yes: 12 per minute while shredding, plus the pollution of every fluid unit it burns.

**Will it work with Factorio 2.1?**
Yes, it will be updated when 2.1 is out as stable.
