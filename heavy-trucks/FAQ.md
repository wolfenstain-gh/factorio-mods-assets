# FAQ

**Do I need Space Age?**
No. Everything works in the base game except the **V3 Launcher**, which needs Space Age.

**Why does it install Heavy Trucks: Autopilot too?**
The Autopilot gives the trucks their window, schedules and truck stops, so it's a required dependency and installs by itself. The trucks can still be driven by hand like any car.

**What is Heavy Trucks: HD graphics?**
Optional. Heavy Trucks ships with standard-resolution graphics to keep the download small; install the HD mods if you want the trucks at twice the resolution. Nothing else changes.

**Fuel ends up in my cargo. Why?**
That's how the base game works with cars: inserters fill the fuel slots first and, once they're full, put the extra fuel in the trunk. To stop it, **filter the cargo slots**: middle-click a slot in the truck window, like on a cargo wagon. Filtered slots only take that item, so the fuel stays in the fuel slots.

**Can I choose what goes into each slot?**
Yes: **middle-click** a slot to filter it to the item on your cursor (or, with an empty hand, to the item in it). Middle-click again to remove the filter.

**Can I limit the trunk like a chest?**
Yes: the **red button next to "Trunk"** in the truck window, then click the first slot to close. Closed slots take nothing in. Right-click the button to remove the limit. (Vehicles can't have the chests' red bar in the base game, so this does the same job.)

**How do I fill or empty the Tanker Truck?**
Park it next to an **ordinary pump**: the pump's arm reaches the tank valve, like with a fluid wagon. Pump towards the truck to fill it, away from it to empty it. It has two separate tanks, so it can carry two different fluids.

**How do I use the Artillery Truck / V3 Launcher / Radar Truck?**
Stop, then **deploy** it (button in its window or **Shift + G**). Deployed, it can't be driven; fold it the same way. The Artillery Truck and the V3 fire in a cone ahead of them: aim by hand with the targeting tool, or turn on **automatic fire** in the window, with target priorities like a turret's.

**Can I add it to an existing save?**
Yes. Research the trucks as usual.

**Will it work with Factorio 2.1?**
Yes, it will be updated when 2.1 is out as stable. Until then, stay on 2.0 for this mod.
