# FAQ

**What does it add?**
For now, the **Chronosphere**, inspired by Command & Conquer: Red Alert 2. It teleports a group of characters and vehicles to another place, even to another planet. More superweapons are on the way.

**Why can't I build a second Chronosphere?**
Superweapons are unique: only **one of each per force**. While you own one, its recipe is locked and a second one cannot be placed. If you pick yours up or lose it, you can build another.

**How do I use it?**
Wait until it is fully charged (its dome opens). Take the **Chronosphere control** from the shortcut bar or with **H**, click the source zone, then the destination. To send it to another planet, pick the destination from remote view.

**My character died when I teleported!**
Living beings die on the trip. Your characters survive on foot only if your force has researched the **Chronosphere** (it adapts their suits). Inside a vehicle, nobody dies.

**Will I lose anything from my vehicles?**
No. Vehicles and characters travel as they are: inventory, ammo, fuel, equipment, quality, health and driver are kept.

**I can't pick the destination.**
It must be an **explored** zone (fogged is fine; black, unexplored map is not), and it cannot overlap the source.

**Does it need Space Age?**
Yes: it is researched on Aquilo and its parts are made in the electromagnetic plant.
