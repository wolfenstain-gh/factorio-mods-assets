# FAQ

**What does it add?**
Two superweapons inspired by Command & Conquer: Red Alert 2. The **Chronosphere** teleports a group of characters and vehicles to another place, even to another planet. The **Nuclear Missile Silo** launches a nuclear missile at any explored spot of its planet. More superweapons are on the way.

**Why can't I build a second Chronosphere or silo?**
Superweapons are unique: only **one of each per force**. While you own one, its recipe is locked and a second one cannot be placed. If you pick yours up or lose it, you can build another.

**How do I use the Chronosphere?**
Wait until it is fully charged (its dome opens). Take the **Chronosphere control** from the shortcut bar or with **Ctrl + H**, click the source zone, then the destination. To send it to another planet, pick the destination from remote view.

**My character died when I teleported!**
Living beings die on the trip. Your characters survive on foot only if your force has researched the **Chronosphere** (it adapts their suits). Inside a vehicle, nobody dies.

**Will I lose anything from my vehicles?**
No. Vehicles and characters travel as they are: inventory, ammo, fuel, equipment, quality, health and driver are kept.

**I can't pick the destination.**
It must be an **explored** zone (fogged is fine; black, unexplored map is not), and it cannot overlap the source: if it is too close, you get a "Too close" message and can pick another spot.

**How do I use the Nuclear Missile Silo?**
Feed it the ingredients of the missile parts, like the vanilla rocket silo. When all 150 parts are done, the silo opens. Take the **Nuclear missile control** from the shortcut bar and click the target. You can pick it from the map or remote view.

**The nuke destroyed my own base!**
It damages everything in 75 tiles of radius, your own buildings too, like the vanilla atomic bomb but much bigger. The circle under your cursor shows the radius before you fire.

**Can I fire it at another planet?**
No: only at explored spots of the silo's own planet.

**Does it need Space Age?**
Yes. The Chronosphere is researched on Aquilo and its parts are made in the electromagnetic plant. The Nuclear Missile Silo needs science up to Vulcanus and its parts are made in the foundry.
