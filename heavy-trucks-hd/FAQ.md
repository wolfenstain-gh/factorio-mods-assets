# FAQ

**What does it do?**
It gives the trucks of Heavy Trucks (Vanilla+) twice the resolution, as they were rendered. Nothing else changes.

**Do I need it?**
No, it's optional. Heavy Trucks ships with standard-resolution graphics to keep the download small. Install this if you want the full detail.

**How do I turn it on?**
Just enable it: Heavy Trucks uses it automatically.

**Does it change my save?**
No. You can add or remove it at any time.
